## 1. Wallet staking (classic)

Every session escrows MOR in the Diamond contract for its duration:

1. The consumer's **hot wallet** holds MOR and calls `openSession`, which pulls the stake
   into the Diamond (an ERC-20 approval + transfer).
2. On `closeSession`, the **unused** portion of the stake returns to the wallet immediately.
3. The **used** portion — the stake that corresponds to the compute actually consumed that
   day — is **time-locked until the next midnight UTC**. This is by design: staked MOR is
   not meant to be recycled into new sessions on the same day it was used.
4. The locked slice lands in `userStakesOnHold` and must be reclaimed with
   `withdrawUserStakes` once it matures. The proxy-router now does this for you: the
   `GET /blockchain/stakes/onhold` and `POST /blockchain/stakes/withdraw` routes expose it,
   and `STAKE_AUTO_CLAIM=true` runs a background claimer so nothing strands in the contract.

The practical consequence: a busy consumer node needs its **full daily staking volume**
in its hot wallet, because today's used stake is not available again until tomorrow.
For a high-volume node that can be a very large hot-wallet balance — which is exactly
what pooled staking removes.

### Session close: what you get back, and when

![What a consumer gets back at session close, and when](/images/session-close-behavior.png)

The close math splits your stake at the moment the session **stopped consuming
compute** (`min(closedAt, endsAt)`), and there is now **no difference in kind** between
an early end and a normal end — only in proportion:

- **Unused stake** (whatever your stake bought beyond the compute you actually used) is
  **returned instantly** in the `closeSession` transaction, and you can re-stake it the
  same day.
- **Used stake** (the stipend for the compute consumed that day) is **day-locked until the
  next midnight UTC**.

| Scenario | Returned instantly | Locked until midnight UTC |
|----------|--------------------|---------------------------|
| End early (used 1h of a 4h session) | ~3h of stake | ~1h of stake |
| End normally, closed same UTC day | little or nothing | ~the whole stake |
| Close **after** the following midnight | everything | nothing (epoch already passed) |

Before this change a normal or late close returned **everything instantly** — that was the
intra-day recycle loophole. Closing it is the whole point: each day's compute now requires
that day's MOR. Note the last row is not a workaround — between `endsAt` and midnight the
stake sits in the still-open session escrow, equally unusable. Midnight UTC is simply the
earliest your used MOR can become liquid again.

The provider is unaffected by any of this: they are paid only for **actual elapsed time**
(`min(closedAt, endsAt) − openedAt`), from the protocol funding account, never from the
slice you get back.

<Note>
On the legacy **wallet** path the locked slice lands in `userStakesOnHold` and needs a
`withdrawUserStakes` call once it matures — turn on `STAKE_AUTO_CLAIM` so the node does that
for you. On the **pool** path there is no claim step at all; see the daily cycle below.
</Note>

## 2. Pooled staking (delegated)

Instead of one hot wallet holding everything, any number of **cold wallets** pre-authorize
a hot consumer node to stake from a shared, purpose-bound pool inside the Diamond:

1. Each cold wallet calls `grantStakingAllowance(hot, cap, expiry)` — a capped, expiring,
   revocable authorization — and then `fundStakingAllowance(hot, amount)` to move MOR into
   the pool. Both are signed by the cold wallet; this is the only time cold keys are used.
2. The hot wallet opens sessions with `openSessionFromPool`. It needs **no MOR balance and
   no ERC-20 approval** — the stake moves from the pool to the session escrow entirely
   inside the Diamond.
3. On close, the stake recycles **back into the pool** (never to the hot wallet), ready for
   the next session.

Custody guarantees, enforced on-chain:

- Pool funds can only ever move to **session stake** or **back to the funder that
  contributed them** — there is no code path that pays the hot wallet.
- A funder can **revoke** (stops new draws immediately) and **withdraw** at any time;
  amounts currently locked in open sessions are queued and paid out automatically as
  sessions close.
- Draws debit funders **first-in, first-out**, with the hot wallet's own self-escrow
  (if any) always debited last.

### How to back a node (cold wallet owners)

Three transactions, signed by your cold wallet **directly against the Diamond contract**
(Basescan *Write as Proxy*, a hardware-wallet UI, or a script). The proxy-router is never
involved and your keys never touch the node. Take the Diamond and MOR token addresses from
[Networks & tokens](/get-started/networks-and-tokens) — never from a chat or a DM.

1. **Approve** the Diamond to pull your MOR (standard ERC-20):
   `MOR.approve(diamond, amount)`.
2. **Grant** the allowance: `grantStakingAllowance(hot, cap, expiry)` — `hot` is the
   node's wallet address, `cap` is the most you will ever fund, `expiry` is a timestamp
   after which new draws stop (`0` = no expiry). Calling it again updates the cap/expiry.
3. **Fund** it: `fundStakingAllowance(hot, amount)` — moves the MOR into the pool
   (a minimum principal applies to keep dust out).

That's it — the daily lock/unlock cycle needs nothing further from you. To check on your
money: `getStakingAllowance(you, hot)` shows your grant, `getPendingWithdrawal(you, hot)`
anything queued to come back.

To exit, again cold-signed, any time:

- `revokeStakingAllowance(hot)` — stops new draws **immediately**; your funds stay
  withdrawable.
- `withdrawStakingAllowance(hot, amount)` — paid instantly from the pool's free balance;
  any slice currently locked in an open session or unmatured day-lock is queued and paid
  out automatically as sessions close. `claimPendingWithdrawals(hot, n)` is a
  permissionless nudge if you'd rather not wait for the pool's next operation.

<Warning>
**Pooled funds are shared liquidity.** Your MOR is commingled with the other funders'
inside one bucket — the contract tracks what you own, not *which* tokens are yours. Two
consequences to understand before funding:

1. **Withdrawal timing depends on the whole pool, not on "your" tokens.** You are paid
   from whatever is liquid right now; if the pool is heavily staked, your withdrawal
   queues until sessions close or day-locks mature. If the hot wallet never operated
   again, the last resort is `settleExpiredSession` — callable **by anyone** once a
   session has been expired for the grace period — so your exit never depends on the
   node's key staying alive. Funds cannot be permanently stranded, but they can wait
   out open sessions plus up to a day of lock.
2. **Another funder's withdrawal can be paid from liquidity your deposit created**, and
   vice versa. The accounting always squares up as sessions close (the pool owes every
   funder exactly their principal), but the *waiting order* is first-come-first-served
   on liquid funds.

Also note the cap is **lifetime**: `cumulativeFundingCap` limits everything you will ever
fund into this grant. Withdrawing does not free up cap headroom — set the cap to your
total intended commitment.
</Warning>

### How to use the pool (consumer node operators)

On the node, the funding source is one environment variable:

```bash
STAKING_FUND_SOURCE=pool
```

| `STAKING_FUND_SOURCE` | Behavior |
|-----------------------|----------|
| `wallet` (default) | Classic behavior — stake from the hot wallet's MOR balance. |
| `pool` | Always stake from the delegated pool. |
| `auto` | Use the wallet while its balance covers the stake, fall back to the pool. |

No other change is needed: the router picks `openSessionFromPool` automatically and the
hot wallet needs MOR only for gas (ETH), not for stake. `STAKE_AUTO_CLAIM` is only
relevant in `wallet`/`auto` modes — pool stake recycles by itself.

To check what's in the pool, ask the router (HTTP Basic Auth, on the private `:8082`
admin port):

```bash
curl -u admin:yourpassword http://localhost:8082/blockchain/pool
```

```json
{
  "availableToStake": "...",      // can the node fund its next session right now?
  "freeBalance": "...",           // pool MOR not locked anywhere
  "lockedBalance": "...",         // in open sessions + maturing day-locks
  "pendingWithdrawalsTotal": "...", // funder exits queued for payout
  "funderCount": "5",
  "holdCount": "1"                // day-lock buckets not yet recycled
}
```

`availableToStake` is the number to alert on: it already counts matured day-locks, so it
answers "can I open the next session?" directly.

### Worked example: five wallets backing one node

![Five wallets funding one pool, intra-day ratchet and midnight reset](/images/pooled-staking-five-wallets.png)

**Setup.** Cold wallets A, B, C, D, E each `grantStakingAllowance(hot, cap, 0)` (no expiry)
and `fundStakingAllowance(hot, 200)`. The pool is now `freeBalance = 1000`, five funders in
**FIFO order A→B→C→D→E**, and the node's hot wallet holds **0 MOR**.

**Intra-day.** Each `openSessionFromPool` draws FIFO, oldest funder first (A is drained
before B, and so on; the hot wallet's own self-escrow, if any, is last). When a session
closes, the **unused** portion recycles to the pool's free balance instantly (re-stakeable
immediately) and the **used** portion is attributed to that session's most-recently-drawn
funders and parked in the day-lock bucket. The visible effect is a ratchet: free balance
steps down by the cumulative *used* stipend across the day, while unused stake churns
freely.

| Time | Event | free | day-locked |
|------|-------|------|------------|
| 09:00 | open S1 (300) → draws A 200 + B 100 | 700 | 0 |
| 10:00 | close S1, used 120 | 880 | 120 |
| 10:05 | open S2 (300) | 580 | 120 |
| 11:30 | close S2, used 90 | 790 | 210 |
| …    | more sessions | … | grows toward 1000 |

So a 1000-MOR pool can back an unlimited number of session *opens* per day but only
**1000 MOR of actually-consumed compute per day** — that ceiling is the intended daily buy
pressure, now spread across five wallets instead of one hot wallet. Peak concurrent stake
is likewise bounded by the current free balance.

**Inter-day (no expiry).** At 00:00 UTC the day-lock bucket matures. `getAvailableToStake`
reports the restored capacity immediately, and the first pool operation of the new day folds
the matured funds back into the free balance in the same transaction — the pool opens Day 2
at `free ≈ 1000` again with grants untouched. This repeats indefinitely with no action from
the cold wallets or the node.

**Exiting.** Any funder can `withdrawStakingAllowance` at any time: paid from free balance
immediately, with any portion momentarily locked in an open session or unmatured bucket
queued FIFO and paid automatically as sessions close and buckets mature. One funder leaving
never disturbs the others, and every wallet only ever gets its own MOR back.

## 3. The daily unlock cycle (automatic)

The same "used stake locks until midnight UTC" rule applies to pool-funded sessions, but
the recycling is fully automatic:

- Every session closed on a given day contributes its used-stipend lock to **one
  day-lock bucket** that matures at the next midnight UTC.
- At midnight, no transaction is needed: `getAvailableToStake` immediately counts the
  matured bucket as available, and the **next pool operation** (a new session open, a
  funder withdrawal, or a close) absorbs it in the same transaction.
- There is **no nightly housekeeping job** for pool mode. A permissionless
  `releasePoolHolds` call exists only as a fallback for pools idle longer than 8 days.

This is the key operational difference from wallet staking, where the matured
`userStakesOnHold` balance sits in the contract until something calls
`withdrawUserStakes` (hence `STAKE_AUTO_CLAIM` for wallet-mode nodes).

## Monitoring

For treasury / operations visibility:

- `getAvailableToStake(hot)` — what the node can stake right now.
- `getStakingPool(hot)` — free balance, locked balance, queued withdrawals, funder count,
  outstanding day-lock buckets.
- `getPoolStakesOnHold(hot)` — matured (auto-recycling) vs. still-locked amounts.
- Event stream: `StakingAllowanceGranted/Funded/Revoked`,
  `AllowanceDebited/Released/HoldCreated/Withdrawn/WithdrawQueued`, `PendingWithdrawalPaid`.
